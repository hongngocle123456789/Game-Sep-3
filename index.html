Round 01 · Stop the release

Three gases to watch.

These gases can rise from everyday equipment and products when they leak or are handled badly.

CFCs: Older Cooling Gases

What: Chemicals that can strongly damage the ozone layer.

From: Older fridges, ACs, foams, and some old aerosol products.

HCFCs: Replacement Cooling Gases

What: Replacements for CFCs that can still damage the ozone layer.

From: Older ACs and refrigeration systems.

Halons: Fire-Suppressing Gases

What: Chemicals used to put out fires that can strongly damage the ozone layer.

From: Older fire extinguishers and fire-protection systems.
